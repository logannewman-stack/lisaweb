What a session is like

  1. 1

    Arrive

    Come as you are. Shoes off, phone away, tea if you would like it.

  2. 2

    We talk

    A few quiet minutes on what brought you here and what you need today.

  3. 3

    Cleansing

    Sage and palo santo to clear the space, and a moment of stillness before we begin.

  4. 4

    Sound

    Bowls, chimes and drum, placed where they are needed. You simply rest and breathe.

  5. 5

    Rest

    Time to come back slowly. Nobody rushes you out.
