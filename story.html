The story behind Strands of Grace

2017 to 2026

Strands of Grace grew one strand at a time. This is the story of how it began, how it has changed, and what it means.

What the name means

  • The strands

    The flowing strands are the threads of a life: separate, colourful, and always connected to one another.

  • The pearls

    A pearl is formed when something difficult is met with patience. Each one is a lesson or a moment, strung together with grace.

  • The lotus

    The lotus rises through muddy water and opens clean and whole, a reminder that growth often begins in the dark.

  • The seven colours

    Seven colours for seven intentions, from love and compassion to grace and light.
