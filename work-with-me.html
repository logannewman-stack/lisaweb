Work with me

Three gentle ways to work together. If you are not sure which is right for you, send me a note and we will find it together.

  • Sound bath

    Small groups, 75 minutes

    Lie back, be wrapped in blankets and let the bowls, chimes and drum do the work. You do nothing but breathe.

    Book a sound bath

  • One-to-one session

    Just you, 60 minutes

    We begin with a conversation. Then sound and stillness, shaped around you and what you need today.

    Book a one-to-one session

  • Cleansing ceremony

    Homes and spaces, 90 minutes

    Sage, palo santo, sound and intention for a home, a studio or a season that is ready for a fresh start.

    Book a cleansing ceremony

What a session is like

  1. 1

    Arrive

    Come as you are. Shoes off, phone away, tea if you would like it.

  2. 2

    We talk

    A few quiet minutes on what brought you here and what you need today.

  3. 3

    Cleansing

    Sage and palo santo to clear the space, and a moment of stillness before we begin.

  4. 4

    Sound

    Bowls, chimes and drum, placed where they are needed. You simply rest and breathe.

  5. 5

    Rest

    Time to come back slowly. Nobody rushes you out.

A minute for you

Not ready to book? Take a minute just for you. Breathe in for four, hold for seven, and breathe out for eight.

The oils I use

The essential oils I diffuse in every session are the same ones I use at home. I share them through doTERRA, and I am always happy to help you choose your first few.

  • Lavender

    Soft, floral and calming.

  • Frankincense

    Warm, grounding and steady.

  • Wild Orange

    Bright, sweet and uplifting.

  • Peppermint

    Cool, crisp and refreshing.

  • Eucalyptus

    Fresh, clean and clearing.
