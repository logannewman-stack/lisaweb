# Strands of Grace: brand guide for the website

Everything here comes from Lisa's own brief (her ChatGPT conversation) and her
logo. It applies to every design option.

## Name and logo
- Business: **Strands of Grace**. Founder: **Lisa Brunson**.
- Tagline (part of the logo): **"Your journey begins with you."**
- The logo is the horizontal lotus-and-pearls design: a watercolour lotus in
  pastel petals outlined in gold, a strand of pearls across it, and the
  lettering in gold and rainbow gradients with flowing swashes.
- Lisa has three logo versions. This is the horizontal one; the other two can
  be swapped in later.

## Rules from her brief
- Warm ivory homepage so the colourful lotus logo stands out. Plum headings,
  champagne gold as a delicate accent, charcoal body text.
- Biography and story areas alternate between ivory and soft cream.
- Lavender, blush and gold are the most prominent accents. Blue, teal, green
  and peach are woven through subtly, echoing the lotus petals and the strands.
- Do not make each section a different chakra colour. The colours flow
  naturally through the site, "reflecting the interconnected strands of your
  life".
- Elegant, peaceful, spiritually welcoming and sophisticated, rather than
  overly colourful.
- Never set small text in a pale pastel on ivory or white. Pastels are for
  shapes, washes, lines, pearls and petals. Text is plum or charcoal.
- Champagne gold fails contrast as text on ivory. Use it for lines, ornaments,
  fills and large display flourishes only; text on a gold fill is plum or
  charcoal.

## Contrast notes (WCAG)
- Deep Plum `#574467` on Warm Ivory: about 8.5:1. Fine at any size.
- Charcoal `#39343D` on Warm Ivory: about 12:1.
- Charcoal on Champagne Gold `#D9B96B`: about 6.4:1, so gold buttons with
  charcoal or plum text pass.
- Ivory text on Deep Plum: about 8.5:1, so plum buttons with ivory text pass.
- Every pastel on ivory is below 2:1. Never use one for text.

## Out of scope
Lisa's brief also describes a future app with a personalised questionnaire.
The app is a separate project and is not part of this website. Nothing on the
site should mention or imitate it.
