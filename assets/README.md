# Where Lisa's files go

Drop her files into these folders, then point to them from `js/content.js`
(or `index.html` for the portrait). Nothing else needs to change.

| Folder | What goes here | Where it is used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/portraits/` | A photo of Lisa (a tall, portrait-orientation photo works best) | `index.html`, the "My story" section |
| `assets/vision-board/` | The vision board photos, one file per item | `js/content.js`, `board` list, `src` |
| `assets/instruments/` | Photos of her instruments | `js/content.js`, `instruments` list, `photo` |
| `assets/tattoo/` | Photos or scans of her tattoo | Reference for weaving the symbols into `logo.svg` |
| `assets/logo.svg` | The mark: lotus over three rings of sound | Favicon, hero, footer |

Keep photos under about 400 KB each. JPG for photos, PNG for anything with
transparency, SVG for the logo. Square or 4:5 crops look best on the board.
