My story

I found sound the way most people do: on my back, on a mat, on a day I did not think anything could reach me. A bowl was struck somewhere behind my head and something in my chest let go. I have been following that sound, and sharing it, ever since.

Smoke came first, though. I grew up with sage in the house, and with the idea that a room could be cleared the same way a heart could, and that the two were never really separate. Palo santo came later, from a teacher who told me to stop trying so hard and start listening.

Today I hold sound baths, work with people one to one, and cleanse homes and studios that are ready for a new chapter. I am not here to fix anyone. I am here to hold a steady space, and let the sound do what it already knows how to do.

Nothing here is complicated. Lie down. Breathe. Let the bowls do the rest.

What a session is like

Every session is a little different, but they all move through the same five moments.

    Sage and palo santo

    Every session starts the same way. The room is cleared with white sage, the way it was done in my family long before I understood why. Then palo santo, sweet and slow, to invite something gentle back in.

    Cleansing is not about getting rid of anything. It is about making room. Room for rest, for the next chapter, for a version of yourself that has been waiting patiently for the noise to die down.

    When you cannot get to a session, there is always this. Four counts in, seven held, eight out.

    The mark

    It started on her wrist. The mark takes the symbols of Lisa's tattoo and sets a lotus among them.

    The lotus
    It grows out of mud and dark water and opens clean. That is the whole story of healing, in one flower.
    The moon and its phases
    The crescent from her wrist, with the moon moving through it. Nothing stays dark for long, and nothing stays full forever.
    The eye
    Watchful and kind. Protection, and the habit of seeing what is really there.
    The rays of dots
    The light above it all, the way her tattoo draws it.
