Welcome to Strands of Grace

Every life is woven from many strands: the people we love, the seasons we walk through, the moments that change us. Strands of Grace is a gentle space to slow down, breathe and gather those strands back together, with kindness.

Whether you are looking for calm, for clarity or simply for a moment that belongs to you, you are welcome here.

Read the story of Strands of Grace

Seven colours, seven intentions

Each petal of the Strands of Grace lotus carries a colour, and each colour carries an intention.

  • Blush Pink Love, compassion and kindness
  • Soft Lavender Spirituality, intuition and reflection
  • Serenity Blue Peace, calm and communication
  • Gentle Teal Balance, harmony and renewal
  • Sage Green Growth, healing and connection
  • Soft Peach Warmth, creativity and new beginnings
  • Champagne Gold Grace, elegance, light and beauty
